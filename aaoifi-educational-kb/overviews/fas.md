# AAOIFI Financial Accounting Standards (FAS) — Overview

*One-line guide. These are accounting rules — how Islamic banks record
transactions in their books — not Shariah rulings. Full simple-English pages
are planned; the list below follows AAOIFI's published FAS 1–26. AAOIFI has
continued issuing FAS beyond 26 (e.g. FAS 33 on investments in sukuk and shares).*

1. **Objective of financial accounting for Islamic banks and financial institutions** — Why Islamic banks prepare accounts: to show Shariah compliance and who has rights to what.
2. **Concept of financial accounting for IFIs** — The basic ideas under the accounts: what counts as an asset, a liability, or income in Islamic finance.
3. **General presentation and disclosure in the financial statements of IFIs** — What an Islamic bank's financial statements must show, including extra Shariah-related disclosures.
4. **Murabaha and Murabaha to the purchase orderer** — How to record cost-plus sales in the books.
5. **Mudaraba financing** — How to record profit-sharing investments where the bank provides the money.
6. **Musharaka financing** — How to record partnership investments.
7. **Disclosure of bases for profit allocation between owners' equity and investment account holders** — How the bank must explain the way it splits profits between itself and its depositors/investors.
8. **Equity of investment account holders and their equivalent** — How to show investment account holders' money, which is neither a regular deposit nor ordinary equity.
9. **Salam and Parallel Salam** — How to record advance-payment purchases of goods to be delivered later.
10. **Ijarah and Ijarah Muntahia Bittamleek** — How to record leases and lease-to-own deals.
11. **Zakah** — How the bank calculates and reports Zakah.
12. **General presentation and disclosure in the financial statements of Islamic insurance companies** — Financial statement rules for Takaful operators.
13. **Provisions and Reserves** — When and how to set aside money for expected losses.
14. **Investment Funds** — How to account for Islamic investment funds.
15. **Provisions and Reserves in Islamic Insurance Companies** — Loss reserves for Takaful operators.
16. **Foreign Currency Transactions and Foreign Operation** — How to handle currency exchange in the books.
17. **Investments** — How to record and value the bank's own investments.
18. **Islamic Financial Services offered by Conventional Financial Institutions** — Rules for conventional banks' Islamic windows.
19. **Contributions in Islamic Insurance Companies** — How to record Takaful contributions (the equivalent of premiums).
20. **Deferred Payment Sale** — How to record sales paid for over time.
21. **Disclosure on Transfer of Assets** — What must be disclosed when assets move, for example in securitizations.
22. **Segment Reporting** — Breaking results down by business line.
23. **Consolidation** — Combining parent and subsidiary accounts into one set.
24. **Investment in Associates** — How to record stakes in companies the bank influences but does not control.

26. *(Reserved in the published series listing)*
