Upcoming events

Islamic finance and Muslim business events in the USA, with links to register. Dates and ticket prices come from the organisers' own pages.

Muslims in Business Conference

Saturday, December 5, 2026 · Boston, MA · Klarman Hall, Harvard Business School

A student-run conference on faith and business, with leaders from technology, finance, healthcare, philanthropy and entrepreneurship. About 1,000 people attended the first edition.

Organiser: Islamic Society, Harvard Business School. Tickets $45–$90. Register on the organiser's site.

MAS Annual Convention 2026 (MASCON26)

December 26–28, 2026 · Chicago, IL · McCormick Place

The largest annual Muslim gathering in North America, with three days of sessions, a youth programme, the USA Quran Competition and a bazaar of 600+ Muslim-owned businesses. This year's theme is Faith Answering the Call: From Action to Impact.

Organiser: Muslim American Society. Tickets $89–$99. Register on the organiser's site.

ICNA-MAS Annual Convention 2027

May 29–31, 2027 · Baltimore, MD · Baltimore Convention Center

One of the largest Islamic conventions in North America, with lectures, workshops and a bazaar of 500+ Muslim-owned businesses. More than 25,000 people attended in 2026.

Organiser: Islamic Circle of North America and Muslim American Society. Ticket prices on the organiser's site. Register on the organiser's site.

AMCOB LEAD Summit 2027

June 5–6, 2027 · Chicago, IL · Venue to be announced

A two-day working summit for Muslim founders, CEOs and executives, capped at 300 seats, with 12 sessions on scaling a business. The 2026 edition ran in Houston.

Organiser: Allied Muslim Chamber of Business (AMCOB). $750 full summit. Register on the organiser's site.
