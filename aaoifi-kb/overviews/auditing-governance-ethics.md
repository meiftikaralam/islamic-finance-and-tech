# AAOIFI Auditing, Governance & Ethics Standards — Overview

*One-line guide. Full simple-English pages are planned.*

## Auditing Standards

1. **Objective and principles of auditing** — What an audit of an Islamic bank aims to check.
2. **The Auditor's Report** — What the audit report must say.
3. **Terms of Audit Engagement** — What the auditor and the bank agree before the audit starts.
4. **Testing for Compliance with Sharia Rules and Principles by an External Auditor** — How the auditor checks Shariah compliance, not just the numbers.
5. **The Auditor's Responsibility to Consider Fraud and Error** — The auditor's duty to watch for fraud and mistakes.

## Governance Standards

1. **Sharia Supervisory Board: Appointment, Composition and Report** — How the board of scholars that certifies compliance is chosen, and what it must report.
2. **Sharia Review** — The periodic check that the bank's activities followed Shariah.
3. **Internal Sharia Review** — The bank's own internal Shariah-checking function.
4. **Audit and Governance Committee for IFIs** — The board committee overseeing audit and governance.
5. **Independence of Sharia Supervisory Board** — Keeping the scholars' board free from management pressure.
6. **Statement on Governance Principles for IFIs** — The overall governance rulebook for Islamic banks.
7. **Corporate Social Responsibility** — The bank's duties to society beyond profit.

## Ethics Standards

1. **Code of ethics for accountants and auditors of IFIs** — Professional ethics for Islamic finance accountants and auditors.
2. **Code of ethics for employees of IFIs** — Conduct rules for staff of Islamic banks.

*Source: AAOIFI standards listings via islamicmarkets.com/education/aaoifi-standards.*
