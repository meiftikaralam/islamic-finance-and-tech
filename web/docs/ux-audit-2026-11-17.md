# UX audit — islamicfintech.org, November 17, 2026

Method: the live site, checked page by page against the Laws of UX checklist
(~/workspace/UX-RULE.md) and standard practice (NN/g heuristics, WCAG contrast).
Counts and ratios below were measured on the live pages, not estimated.

## What is working

- One H1 on every main page (home, newsletters, posts, AAOIFI, glossary, editions, posts).
- Article reading is comfortable: Source Serif at 20px with 1.85 line height.
- Main tap targets pass the 44px guideline: subscribe buttons 48px, month chips 44px.
- Contrast passes where it matters most: body grey #666 on white is 5.7:1, the dark
  featured card's text is 7:1.
- Every page is prerendered, so content and navigation work with JavaScript off.
- The home page puts the newest editions first and articles end with the subscribe
  box — the two positions people remember (Serial Position).

## High priority

1. **Editions are dead ends.** An edition page links to no other edition. After
   finishing one, the reader's only onward path is the subscribe box. A daily
   reader's natural next step is the previous edition. Fix: previous / next
   edition links at the foot of every edition page.

2. **AAOIFI and Glossary are hidden on mobile.** The phone nav shows Daily
   Newsletters and Posts; the other four sections sit behind the hamburger, and
   the new home page links to neither section. Two core assets depend on a menu
   many visitors never open. Fix: link both from the home page (a short link row
   under Most popular posts is enough), and consider a slimmer 4-link bar.

## Medium priority

3. **The orange fails text contrast.** #F97316 on white measures 2.8:1; WCAG AA
   asks 4.5:1 for text this size. The orange carries meaning in the active nav
   tab, the TODAY marker, and the "Latest edition" pill (white text on orange,
   also 2.8:1). Fix: use orange-700 #C2410C for text and the pill — 5.2:1 both
   ways — and keep the bright orange only for large fills.

4. **Small green text is under contrast.** Brand-600 #16A34A measures 3.3:1 and is
   used at 13–14px for the SS numbers on the AAOIFI index, glossary source links,
   and back links. Fix: brand-700 (#14532D, 9.1:1) for all small text; brand-600
   stays for large headings.

5. **AAOIFI standard pages missed the article treatment.** They render with no
   summary box, no byline, and no subheadings at all (zero H2/H3 on the SS 1
   page), so each standard reads as one unbroken run and looks plainer than an
   edition. Fix: give standard pages the same head as editions (title + the
   one-line summary in a box), and promote the summaries' own sections (what it
   is, key rules, example) to real H2 headings in the knowledge-base files.

6. **The About page has no H1.** It is the only main page without one (measured:
   zero). The How it's made page uses the same component and needs the same
   check. Fix: render the page title as an H1 in the MdPage component, the same
   pattern the article pages now use.

7. **The glossary page is heavy.** Its HTML alone is 235 KB, and the shared
   JavaScript bundle is large enough that the build warns about it (over
   500 KB). On a slow phone connection the page arrives late. Fix when time
   allows: paginate or lazy-load the glossary list, and split the content out
   of the main bundle. A watch item, not a fire.

## Low priority

8. **Two subscribe forms on the home page** — the hero form plus the site-wide
   bottom box. Mild duplication; hiding the bottom box on the home page only
   would clean it up.

9. **Article lines run long.** At 720px wide and 20px serif, a line holds
   roughly 75–85 characters; reading research prefers 50–75. Narrowing the
   article column to about 660px would help a little. Taste-level.

10. **Filters give no count feedback.** Filtering the glossary or the standards
    shows the matches with no "3 of 62" line. A small count line is cheap
    feedback (Postel: clear feedback for any input).

11. **Nav text links are about 24px tall**, under the 44px guideline. Errors are
    rare with this few links, but padding the two bar links up to 44px costs
    nothing.

12. **A most-popular post has no subheadings.** The tokenised sukuk post runs
    over a thousand words with zero H2 headings. Adding subheads is content
    work for its next edit.

## Suggested order

Previous/next edition links and home-page links to AAOIFI and Glossary first
(they change what readers can reach), then the two contrast fixes (one-line
colour swaps), then the AAOIFI standard page treatment and the About H1.
The rest can ride along with future edits.
